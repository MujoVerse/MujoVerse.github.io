---
title: Drive a mech (MinePilot)
description: "Climb into a robot and drive it with keyboard and mouse."
---

With MinePilot installed, every T2 or G1 is your mech: climb in and drive it with your usual movement keys. Every step is a real step, so it sways on rough ground and falls when hit.

## Getting in and out

- Walk up to a robot and **right-click it with an empty hand**, look at it and press **F**, or run `/minepilot drive` (robot within 6 blocks).
- Press **G** or run `/minepilot leave` to let go.
- One driver per robot at a time.

## Controls

| Key | Effect |
|---|---|
| WASD | walk at 1.5 m/s; sideways speed is capped at 1 m/s |
| Sprint | forward up to 3 m/s |
| Sneak | fine movement at 0.5 m/s |
| Mouse | turns the head first; past the neck limit the body turns too |
| F5 | first / third person |
| Left-click the ground | **point-to-go**: the robot walks to the spot under the crosshair |
| `=` / `-` | speed for the next point-to-go |
| **H** | request get-up after a fall |

All keys can be rebound under Options → Controls → Key Binds → MinePilot.

The HUD shows the control mode, requested and measured speed, posture and target distance. Abilities a robot lacks are marked "unavailable"; for example, the G1 has no head control.
